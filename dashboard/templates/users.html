Role Permission Matrix
Administrator:

Full access to system operations: upload PCAP captures, triage incidents, add/remove user accounts, and change operator roles.

Analyst:

Operational triage: upload authorized PCAPs, review forensic packets, update incident status (new, investigating, resolved, false_positive), and record investigation notes.

Viewer:

Read-only audit: inspect alerts, view analytics charts, download evidence PCAPs, and export CSV reports. Upload and triage modifications are disabled.
